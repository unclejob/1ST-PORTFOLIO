From the kitchen and the factory floor to data and AI

I started my career in operations — production scheduling and inventory at Vigil Milk Plc, machinery and quality checks at General Import and Distribution, and kitchen operations and food safety at The Place Restaurant. Those roles taught me process thinking, accuracy under pressure, and how to coordinate across departments to hit a deadline.

A B.Sc. in Mathematics and Computer Science gave me the tools to improve those processes with data. Today I work as a Data Analyst, turning messy operational data into decisions with SQL, PostgreSQL, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau. I am now building on that base with AI — exploring machine learning, LLMs, and RAG — with a particular interest in applying data and AI to agriculture and food systems in Africa.

Right now: learning in public, building small AI/ML mini-projects, and looking for my next challenge.

Operations first

Production control, quality testing, safety compliance, and kitchen management trained me to spot bottlenecks before they become costly mistakes.

Data second

Math, SQL, and BI turned those observations into decisions that stakeholders could act on.

AI next

I am layering machine learning and LLM tooling on top of my analytical foundation to solve real-world problems in food and agriculture.
